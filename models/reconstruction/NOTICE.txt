This experiment uses the 1,797-image optdigits.tes subset distributed by
scikit-learn's load_digits. Original intensities 0–16 are normalized to 0–1.
The subset is split into new stratified training/test partitions (seed 73),
not the original UCI writer-disjoint training/test protocol. The gallery
contains 20 images from this experiment's test partition. Interactive masks,
hand-edited pixels, model reconstructions, embeddings and visualizations are
transformations made for this educational experiment by Huiyu Chen's blog.
No endorsement by the dataset creators is implied.
