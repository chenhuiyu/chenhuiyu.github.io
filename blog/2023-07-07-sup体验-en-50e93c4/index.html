Stand Up Paddle Boarding (SUP)

Stand-Up Paddleboarding (SUP) is a water sport that originated from modern surfing in Hawaii. Surfers stand on a board that floats on the water and use a paddle to propel themselves through the water. This sport has rapidly grown in popularity around the world in recent years and has become one of many people's favorite water activities.

Experience 🌊

If you’re interested in stand-up paddle boarding, consider taking a dedicated course to learn about and experience the activity. I recently took a stand-up paddle boarding lesson on the east coast of Singapore and here is my experience.

The course lasted two hours, and the instructor introduced the basic knowledge and skills of SUP in great detail. They explain how to balance while standing on the board, properly use the paddle to propel yourself through the water, and how to steer and control the direction of the board. Although I felt a little unsteady at first, I quickly got used to the standing position and was able to easily master the technique of using the paddle.

The course arrangement is very reasonable and suitable for beginners. With the guidance of my instructor, I was able to pick it up quickly and only fell into the ocean once 😅, which I felt a little embarrassed about. However, SUP is a very fun and challenging activity, and falling into the water is part of the learning process. I believe that with practice, I can become more proficient and stable.

The course takes place in the open ocean on the east coast, which means we have free surfing in the open ocean. This open environment makes people feel very comfortable and free. However, we also need to pay attention to sun protection ☀️ as prolonged exposure to the sun can cause damage to the skin.

Registration information 📝

If you are interested in stand up paddle boarding lessons, you can sign up at:https://www.onepa.gov.sg/courses/sup-starter-course-open-water-c026977237. This is a course offered by an institution in Singapore called PAssion WaVe @ East Coast. The registration fee is SGD 61 💰.

There are some course requirements that need to be met before enrolling

. You need to have basic swimming ability 🏊‍♂️ and be able to swim at least 50 meters in the sea while wearing a life jacket. This is to ensure your safety during water activities.

The course location is located at PAssion WaVe @ East Coast, Singapore. The specific address is 1390 ECP, Singapore 468961 📍. This location is perfect for stand up paddle boarding as it offers open water and suitable conditions.

Stand up paddle boarding is an exciting and thrilling water activity that is a great choice for both surfing enthusiasts and beginners. By taking lessons, you will be able to learn proper technique and safety, enjoy surfing, and make great memories in the ocean. If you are interested in this activity, consider signing up for a lesson and start your stand up paddle boarding journey! 🏄‍♂️🌊
