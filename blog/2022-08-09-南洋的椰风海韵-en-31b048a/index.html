The gentle coconut wind blows on the seaside of Nanyang,
Emerald green palms dance in the blue sky.
The waves of the sea, like songs and tears, echo in the ears.
Between the ebb and flow of the tide, the distant sound of the waves resounds like the rhyme of poetry,

Along the coastline, white waves gently hug the beach,
The fine grains of sand gently moisten your feet.
The rocks in the distance, quietly gazing at the endless vastness of the sea,
Like an ancient wise man guarding secrets.

As night falls, the stars scatter across the sky like bright jewels.
The sea breeze brings the moonlight, pouring down like the Milky Way,
The swaying coconut trees bring into boundless poetry and painting,
Dreams and hopes intertwined into beautiful pictures,
Yongming Nanyang coconut breeze and sea charm, soul station.
